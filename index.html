The Amelia Vollers House is a historic Queen Anne Victorian cottage near downtown San Mateo, California.

The house was built for the Vollers in 1891 by James Sharpe Tannahill, who also built homes in Redwood City and Menlo Park, and who went on to build the Kearney mansion in Fresno.
